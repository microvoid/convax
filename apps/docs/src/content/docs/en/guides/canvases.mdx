A canvas stores cards and their connections. Its tab is a view of that saved canvas: closing a tab preserves the graph so you can reopen it later.

## Navigate and arrange

Choose **Touchpad** or **Mouse** in the lower-left controls. macOS defaults to Touchpad: two-finger scrolling moves the canvas and pinching zooms. Other platforms default to Mouse, with wheel zoom and drag-to-pan. Your explicit preference is remembered across canvases and client restarts.

Drag a card's name area to move it. Alignment guides help match neighboring edges and centers; a multi-card selection keeps its relative positions. Use the external lower-right grip to resize a card. Expanded content scrolls inside the card; native editors and Chat history keep their own scrolling and text selection.

When the canvas owns focus:

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Select all cards | Cmd/Ctrl+A |
| Copy, paste, duplicate | Cmd/Ctrl+C, Cmd/Ctrl+V, Cmd/Ctrl+D |
| Group / ungroup | Cmd/Ctrl+G / Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+G |
| Move selected cards or groups | Arrow keys |
| Remove selected cards or connections | Delete or Backspace |
| Undo / redo graph edits | Cmd/Ctrl+Z / Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z |
| Fit canvas | Cmd/Ctrl+0 |

Inputs, conversation controls, terminals, menus, and IME composition retain their native keys. Undo changes the graph; it does not rewind sent messages or filesystem writes.

## Group cards

Select at least two cards, then choose **Group selection** in the selection toolbar or context menu. Drag the group title to move the whole group, with alignment guides. The frame follows the current sizes of its cards; you can still edit, move, and resize each card independently.

Double-click the group title or choose **Rename group** to rename it. A card’s context menu offers **Add to group** and **Remove from group**. Moving across a frame does not change membership. Groups cannot contain other groups, fold, or resize their contents together.

**Ungroup** keeps the cards and their connections. **Delete group and contents** removes the members; deleting Chats retains the existing stop-and-archive confirmation. Copying or duplicating all group members preserves the name under a new group identity. Copying only part of a group makes independent cards. Group edits support graph undo and redo.

## Manage saved canvases

Drag a top tab to reorder it, or double-click its title to rename the canvas. The workspace menu opens **All canvases**, with search and separate active and archived lists.

- **Archive** closes the tab without stopping its conversations. Unarchive makes it available to reopen.
- **Duplicate canvas** creates independent card and connection identities. File paths remain references; original conversations and drafts are not copied.
- **Permanently delete…** requires confirmation. It removes the canvas and its undo history, stops and archives its conversations, and preserves original files and Session history.

If deletion is unfinished, use its saved **Retry** action.

## Find running work

**Activity** collects completion and pending-interaction notices. Clicking a notice opens its canvas and reveals the corresponding Chat thread; only successful navigation marks it read. **Automation** opens DSH's task manager over the canvas and can locate the conversation linked to a task.
